The plan

Here's what we're building

Three steps, in the order we're building them.

STEP 01

Tell us your business

Industry, state, and size. We map the specific rules that actually apply to you — not a generic regulatory firehose.

STEP 02

We watch, so you don't have to

Agency sites, state boards, and industry bodies, checked continuously and filtered down to your profile only.

STEP 03

You get plain English, not legalese

What changed, why it matters, and what — if anything — to do about it, with a deadline attached when there is one.

RegWatch is being designed as a monitoring aid, not a substitute for legal advice. For anything high-stakes, loop in a licensed professional.

Questions

Before you join

Is RegWatch live yet?

No — we're validating the idea and figuring out what to build first. Joining early access puts you first in line and lets you help shape it.

How is this different from just reading agency newsletters myself?

Mostly speed and filtering. The plan is to watch sources continuously and only surface what applies to your specific profile, in plain English, instead of you scanning a general feed for the one item that matters.

Will RegWatch replace my compliance lawyer?

No. It's being designed as a monitoring aid that tells you when something changed and points you in the right direction — not as legal advice. For anything high-stakes, you'll still want a licensed professional.

Is my information private?

We'll only use your email to share updates about RegWatch as we build it. You can unsubscribe at any point.

What if my industry isn't listed above?

Tell us anyway when you join. That's genuinely useful — it helps us prioritize what gets built first.
